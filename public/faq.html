File Sorter FAQ

A human-in-the-loop assistant that reads inbound email attachments, suggests which case and folder they belong in, and waits for your approval before anything is saved to Dropbox.

Purpose & goals

The problem: Client documents arrive by email from all kinds of senders — medical records vendors, HR departments, e-sign services, relatives, listservs. Sorting them into the right case folder takes time and context.

What File Sorter does: It reads each email and attachment, uses AI to suggest a case and Dropbox subfolder, and posts a review card in Slack so a team member can confirm or correct before filing.

What it never does: It does not automatically save files. Every document requires an explicit Approve click. Low-confidence or ambiguous items are flagged for human review.

Human approval required. Think of the bot as a smart intake clerk — it prepares the filing recommendation; you make the final call.

How it works

1
Email arrives with attachment(s) Gmail / Workspace forwards mail with attachments to the File Sorter webhook.
2
AI analyzes the content Reads subject, body (including forwards), filename, and document text. Matches against known cases and staff hints.
3
One Slack card per email Multiple attachments from the same email appear on a single card. If case confidence is 95% or higher, the card is posted to that case’s Slack channel (not #file-sorter-queue) and @mentions staff from the channel topic.
4
You review in Slack Check the suggested case, folder, and confidence. Use buttons or thread commands to correct if needed.
5
Approve → saved to Dropbox All attachments in the email are filed. The case Slack channel gets a confirmation with the file attached.

What you see on a queue card

  • From / To / Subject / Received — email metadata
  • Attachment(s) — filename(s) and each suggested folder
  • AI Suggested Case — matched client case (Slack channel name), or Intake — no case folder yet for new-client mail from intake@ramosjames.com
  • AI Suggested Folder — Dropbox subfolder (Medical, Pleadings, Intake, etc.)
  • Confidence — separate Case, Folder, and Overall scores. Case below 75% → needs review.
  • Reason — short explanation of why it chose that case/folder

Cards marked Needs Human Review usually mean no case matched, low case confidence, or the AI couldn't identify the PI client from the email alone. Adobe Sign and other e-sign mail is treated like normal client documents — not intake — unless it came through intake@ramosjames.com.

Slack buttons

These appear on each queue card. Clicking a button is how you interact with the bot — there are no slash commands.

Primary action
Approve

Saves all attachments in this email to Dropbox under the suggested (or overridden) case and folder. Posts confirmation to the case Slack channel with the file attached.

Get help
Change Case/Folder

Posts instructions in the thread for case:, folder:, and Teach Case / Teach Folder lines. Does not change anything by itself — reply in the thread, then click Approve.

Flag
Needs Attention

Marks the item for follow-up. You can still use Change Case/Folder and Approve when ready. Use when something looks off but you aren't ready to file or skip.

Skip
Do Not Sort

Closes the item without saving to Dropbox. Use for newsletters, spam, or mail that isn't a client document.

Thread commands

Before clicking Approve, reply in the card's thread. Overrides apply to this email only; Teach lines are saved for future mail (see Teach the system).

Override case or folder (this email only)

case: 1277 case: Juan Garcia folder: Medical folder: MRI-report.pdf | Medical folder: accident-photo.jpg to Photos
  • case: — case number or client name (must match the case index)
  • folder: Medical — one folder for every attachment on the card
  • folder: filename | Folder or folder: filename to Folder — folder for one attachment (use the filename or a unique partial match)

Click Change Case/Folder on the card for the same instructions in Slack.

Dropbox folders

Each case folder in Dropbox has standard subfolders. The AI picks from these lists based on document type and whether the case is pre-lit or in litigation (from the case Slack channel topic).

Core folders (most cases)

Use these for pre-lit, intake, and general filing.

Correspondence Expenses Intake Investigation LOP Lost Wages Medical PD Photos Settlement Subrogation

Litigation folders

Used when the case Slack channel topic indicates the case is in litigation (discovery, trial, etc.). The AI avoids these for pre-lit / intake cases unless the document is clearly litigation-related.

Correspondence Litigation Demand Deposition Discovery Experts Mediation Pleadings Trial
Custom folders: If none of the standard labels fit, you can reply with folder: Your Folder Name. The folder is created in Dropbox on Approve if it doesn't exist yet.

Teach the system

Teach Case and Teach Folder lines tell the AI how to handle future similar mail. Add them in the card thread, then click Approve — hints are saved when you approve, not when you type them.

Teach Case — who is the client? Which case?

Teach Case: Maria Garcia is Juan Garcia's daughter. Teach Case: Cause number DC-24-12345 belongs to case 1277.
  • Relatives, nicknames, or senders who email on behalf of a PI client
  • Cause / docket numbers linked to a case number
  • Treated as authoritative when the same facts appear in a later email

Teach Folder — where should this type of mail go?

Teach Folder: MoveDocs records belong in Medical. Teach Folder: Law360 newsletters should not be sorted. Teach Folder: Photos from this sender belong in Photos.
  • Provider or vendor behavior (which subfolder to use)
  • Recurring non-client mail — pair with Do Not Sort when appropriate
  • Sender-specific folder rules for a known case

What the bot learns automatically on Approve

You don't need a Teach line for every correction. When you Approve, the system may also save hints when:

  • You used case: or folder: because the AI's suggestion was wrong
  • A Cause number in the email or attachment maps to the case you filed to
  • Filename patterns repeat (e.g. medical records, LOP, demand letters) for that case

It does not blindly link every sender to one case — senders often handle many clients. Use explicit Teach Case lines for those relationships.

Legacy: case hint: Legacy: sort hint: Legacy: hint:

Older hint formats still work; prefer Teach Case: and Teach Folder: in new replies.

Multiple attachments

One email with several attachments produces one Slack card, not one card per file. One Approve files everything to the same case.

  • The AI classifies each attachment separately, so different files can have different suggested folders (shown as filename.pdf → Medical on the card).
  • If the first attachment identifies the case, later attachments in the same email usually inherit that case.
  • A thread folder: line forces one folder for all files on the card — don't use it when you want split folders.

Different folders on the same card

When an email has, for example, medical records and photos, the bot can suggest Medical for one file and Photos for another. You do not need a special command for that — it is the default when the AI gets each suggestion right.

  1. Check the Attachments list and each row under Per attachment for the → folder next to every filename.
  2. Do not reply with folder: … in the thread unless every file should go to the same place.
  3. Click Approve once — each file saves to its own suggested subfolder in Dropbox.

If one file has the wrong folder but the others look correct, reply in the thread with a per-file override, then Approve:

folder: MRI-report.pdf | Pleadings folder: accident-photo.jpg to Photos

Use the exact filename or a unique partial name (same rules as skip:). You can add one line per file that needs a different folder.

# Default — AI suggests different folders; Approve files each one MRI-report.pdf → Medical accident-photo.jpg → Photos # Override one file only folder: MRI-report.pdf | Pleadings # Override every file to the same folder folder: Medical

Frequently asked questions

How do I file different attachments to different folders on one card?

Usually you don't need to do anything special. Each attachment is analyzed on its own, and the card shows a folder suggestion per file (e.g. labs.pdf → Medical, id-front.jpg → Photos). Click Approve once and each file goes to its own subfolder under the same case.

Important: a thread line like folder: Medical overrides every attachment on that card. Leave that out when you want split folders.

If only one file has the wrong folder, reply in the thread before Approve, for example: folder: labs.pdf | Pleadings or folder: photo.jpg to Photos. Add one line per file you need to correct. See Multiple attachments for examples.

Why didn't the bot pick a case?

Common reasons: the PI client isn't named clearly in the email (sender is a vendor, not the client), the case isn't in the index yet, or the name only partially matches a different client. Reply with case: for this email, add a Teach Case: line for next time, then Approve.

What is "Intake — no case folder yet"?

That label appears on mail from intake@ramosjames.com (or forwarded from it) when no existing case matched yet — typical new-client intake. Suggested folder is usually Intake. Once a case folder exists and matches the client name, normal case matching applies. Adobe Sign and other signature requests are not treated as intake unless they came through the intake mailbox.

How do Teach Case and Teach Folder work?

Reply in the thread with lines starting with Teach Case: or Teach Folder:, then Approve. They are stored as matching hints and fed into future AI classification. Teach Case helps identify the PI client or link Cause numbers to a case. Teach Folder helps route document types or senders to the right subfolder. See the Teach the system section for examples.

What's the difference between Needs Attention and Needs Human Review?

Needs Human Review on the card title means the AI flagged uncertainty before anyone clicked anything — usually no case match or case confidence below 75%. Needs Attention (status) can also mean someone pressed the button, or a duplicate file was detected in Dropbox.

Can I file to a case the AI didn't suggest?

Yes. Reply in the thread with case: 1277 or case: Client Name, then Approve. If the AI had the wrong case, a correction hint may be saved automatically. For ongoing sender→client relationships, add an explicit Teach Case: line.

What's the difference between core and litigation folders?

Core folders (Medical, Correspondence, Investigation, etc.) are for everyday pre-lit filing. Litigation folders (Pleadings, Discovery, Deposition, Trial, etc.) are used when the case Slack channel topic shows the case is in litigation. Override anytime with folder: Pleadings (or any standard label) in the thread before Approve.

What about Google Drive links in an email?

Drive links in the email body are detected and listed on the queue card, but the bytes can't be downloaded automatically. Classify case/folder as usual, then download the file manually and file it — or use Approve only for attachments the bot actually received.

What emails are ignored?

Emails without attachments or external file links are skipped. Calendar invites (.ics) are skipped. Certain known list senders (e.g. TTLA listserv) are ignored at ingestion. Use Teach Folder: + Do Not Sort for other recurring non-client mail.

Where do files go after Approve?

Files upload to the case's Dropbox folder under the chosen subfolder (Medical, Pleadings, etc.). A summary message with the file is cross-posted to that case's Slack channel.

Does the bot read old Slack messages to update?

No. Only new inbound emails get the latest card layout and logic. Existing queue cards won't change after a deploy.

Settings

Daily scoreboard email (Mon–Fri). Uses the Google service account with Workspace impersonation to send as a firm address. Recipients, From, and send time can be changed here without a redeploy.
